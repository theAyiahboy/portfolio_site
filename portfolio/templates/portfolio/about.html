A little about me

Technology makes more sense when it helps people.

I’m Giovanni Ayiah-Mensah, a Business Information Systems student and aspiring full-stack developer based in Ghana. I’m interested in the space where people, business, and technology meet.

My perspective

A practical path into tech.

Studying Business Information Systems has shaped how I think about software. Good technology is more than code: it should respond to a real need, make a task easier, and work well for the people who rely on it.

I’m building my development skills through hands-on practice. I work with Python and Django on the back end, and HTML, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS on the front end. Each project gives me a chance to connect what I’m learning with something concrete.

I’m still early in my journey, and that’s part of what I enjoy. I’m curious, open to feedback, and motivated by the process of making an idea clearer and more useful with every iteration.
